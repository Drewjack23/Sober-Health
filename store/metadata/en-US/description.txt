Sober Plus Health brings recovery, fitness, nutrition, and everyday wellness into one calm, private experience.

Build a plan around your real goals:

• Track private recovery progress and daily check-ins
• Explore workouts for your time, interests, and equipment
• Discover familiar, goal-aware recipes with complete instructions
• Build custom bowls with live nutrition estimates
• Plan a week of meals and create a combined grocery list
• Log weight, water, mood, activity, meals, and habits
• See trends and milestones without judgment or perfection pressure
• Keep sensitive notification language private by default

Sober Plus Health is designed for adults and provides general wellness information. It does not provide medical care, diagnosis, treatment, detox guidance, or emergency services.
